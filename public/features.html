Product overview

Manage your shared mess without spreadsheets

Track meals, bazar, deposits, rent and utility costs, then calculate monthly settlements from one shared workspace.

Smart Mess Manager is a web-based tool for hostels, apartments and shared mess households. It helps managers and members keep meal, bazar and shared costs organized across a month.

Open Smart Mess Manager

What you can do

Shared mess member and meal tracking

Track everyday meals for each mess member, including partial meals, while keeping monthly totals organized.

Bazar costs and deposits

Keep market purchases and shared grocery costs together so monthly meal calculations have clear source records.

Rent and utility expense records

Record deposits, room costs, rent and utility expenses separately from daily meal entries.

Monthly meal rates and settlements

Review meal rates, payable amounts and member balances for transparent month-end settlements.

Offline-first records and cloud sync

Keep working from local browser records when offline, then synchronize permitted changes when connected.

Monthly reporting and exports

Review past monthly data and generate printable or downloadable reports for shared accounting.

How it works

  1. Create a mess workspace and invite members.
  2. Record daily meals and market expenses as they occur.
  3. Review deposits, shared bills and month-end balances.

Privacy and data

Member balances, deposits and workspace records belong to authenticated accounts. Public search pages do not display those records.
